# Backlinks and outreach for carvcam.com

From the competitor research checked on 2026-10-27. This is off-site work: nothing here has been posted.

## Priorities

1. **Sienci Labs**: request a listing in the software table (80+ programs) and the Toolchain Wizard. Sienci is the most relevant domain for LongMill searches.
2. **Lists and wikis** (GitHub pull requests): awesome-cnc, the UGS software comparison wiki and FabLab CAM tools.
3. **Blogs with outdated facts**: several 2026 'best free CNC software' articles still say free Carbide Create exports for any machine, or quote old Easel prices. Offer the correction with a source and propose adding CarvCAM.
4. **YouTube creators** who teach LongMill: offer a review copy when the installer is ready.
5. **Directories at launch**: AlternativeTo (as an alternative to Easel, VCarve and Carbide Create) and Product Hunt.
